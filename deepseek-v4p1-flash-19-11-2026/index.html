THE RULES OF LIGHT

HOW TO PLAY

You are the last light.

Survive an endless tide of hollow things. They drop motes; collect them to level up and choose a blessing. Stack blessings into absurd, beautiful builds. Every fifth wave, a cosmic horror wakes up.

Graze to bloom

Skim past hostile fire without being hit to charge your Nova. When it is full, unleash it to erase every bullet nearby and tear a hole in the dark.

Dash through death

Your dash grants brief invulnerability. Charges refill automatically. Panic is not a plan, but it is a start.

Chase the combo

Kill quickly or lose the streak. Combos multiply your score and turn a good run into a legendary one.

Die. Grow. Return.

Every run leaves stardust. Spend it in the Constellation to permanently sharpen your light. Death is a fertilizer.

Field notes

  • Motes drift home to you. Keep fighting; you will not lose your harvest.
  • Bosses telegraph. Red lines are promises, not warnings.
  • Save your Nova for the moment the screen blooms with fire.
  • Every run, however short, feeds the next.
