HOW TO SURVIVE THE NIGHT

THE PACT

Armies of the Order of Dawn descend to burn you. Survive until 20:00 and slay the Seraph of Dawn to claim the night. Weapons fire on their own — your only jobs are to move, to choose wisely, and to raise what falls.

THE DEAD SERVE YOU

Every foe that dies near you may rise again as a skeleton under your command. Grow the horde. Shield yourself with a wall of bones. Even Champions leave corpses worth taking.

MOVEMENT

  • WASD / arrow keys — move (hold the mouse to walk toward it, or use the touch joystick)
  • SPACE — Danse Macabre: raise every corpse in sight and chill the living
  • P / ESC — pause and review your build
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — choose a dark pact when you level up · R — reroll (if bought)

THE LONG GAME

Souls level you up. Elites drop relic chests. Max a weapon and pair it with its kindred passive to unlock an EVOLUTION. Every run — win or lose — pays essence for the Dark Altar's permanent powers, and two more souls await your summons.
