HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

  • WASD / Arrows — drift
  • Mouse — aim
  • Space — dash (brief invulnerability)
  • Hold F — siphon: devour matter, feed your body

CAST

  • Left click / J — IGNITE fireball
  • Right click / K — QUENCH water spray
  • E — ARC lightning (conducts through water & metal)
  • Q — SEED spore bloom

THE ELEMENTS

  • EMBER — burns the world, fears water
  • TIDE — drowns fire, cools lava, swims
  • SPARK — swift, electrifies, conducts
  • BLOOM — grows, mends you, burns slowly

THE RULE

Your body is whichever element you have fed most — and it changes the rules. Rock feeds your current body; lava makes you EMBER, water makes you TIDE, spores make you BLOOM. Become the thing you eat.

THE GOAL

Find the Heart at the bottom of every stratum and touch it to descend. Each stratum grants a mutation — permanently, for that run.
