THE RULE OF MASS

You devour anything lighter than you. Anything heavier resists — you cannot swallow a Warden, but you can pick up a mountain and throw it at one.

Your gravity field bends enemy fire. Let them shoot into the void and watch the curve swallow the round.

THE HUNGER

Stop devouring and your mass begins to rot away. After four seconds you shrink; after eight you start starving. Keep eating or die hungry.
